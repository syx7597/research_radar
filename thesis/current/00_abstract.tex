\chapter*{摘\quad 要}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{摘要}

知识图谱问答通过可执行的查询操作连接自然语言问题与外部知识，为小语言模型处理多步推理任务提供了有效途径。模型在自由执行过程中会遇到偏离参考路径的中间状态，而常规成功轨迹训练对这类状态的覆盖较少，容易出现无效调用累积和求解中断。雷达技术资料中的参数还与型号、部件及工作条件紧密关联，要求问答过程准确保持条件关系并提供可定位的来源。针对上述问题，本文研究基于真实执行反馈的恢复轨迹监督方法，并开展雷达知识组织与领域问答实验。

首先，构建面向雷达资料问答的来源知识表示与评价资源。以主体、部件、属性、量值形式、适用条件和来源位置为核心组织记录，保留表格中的多主体归属与条件关联，形成资料归档、记录整理、问题编制和交叉审阅流程。八个制造商来源组共整理 502 条来源读法，编制 96 道中文问题，并通过 AI 交叉审阅建立参考答案与分项评价材料，为领域问答中的事实选择、条件保持和引用支持提供统一评价基础。

其次，提出基于真实偏离状态与可执行后缀的恢复轨迹监督方法。由初始模型在训练问题上自由运行，从失败轨迹中构造 418 个配对样本；保留共同成功前缀、首次参考分歧动作及其执行反馈，通过中间结果句柄重映射连接参考后缀，逐步重放检验执行一致性，并将训练损失集中于后续求解动作。基于 Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct 进行 LoRA 微调，以相同初始模型、训练问题、记录次序和监督 token 预算下的普通续训作为主要对照。在由 KQA Pro 划分的 2,000 题项目留出集上，两次配对续训的平均准确率由 78.75\% 提高至 83.50\%，增量为 4.75 个百分点，问题级配对 bootstrap 的 95\% 置信区间为 $[3.53,5.98]$。两个续训种子分别提升 4.65 和 4.85 个百分点；相对普通分步模型，累计输入与生成 token 总量分别减少 8.84\% 和 4.17\%。状态转移分析进一步表明，收益主要伴随未完成题目的减少，同时保留了完成题之间的正向净改善。

最后，建立由接口适配、记录选择和来源回答组成的雷达问答流程。共同合成接口适配使五个检查点在合成留出任务上达到 39--40/40 的记录选择正确数；四来源问答实验中，普通组与恢复组的正文正确数为 15--16/24，表现接近。围绕领域回答中的参数归属问题，进一步设计保持相同语义记录的条件绑定表示。在固定 96 题实验中，相对平铺表示，正文正确数由 73 提高至 80，绑定错误由 17 降至 11。事后采用统一的精确引用解析规则后，两组联合正确数分别为 55 和 68，表明条件组织与引用处理共同影响答案的证据完整性。

本文形成了从真实执行轨迹构造训练目标、到专业资料组织与分层问答评价的研究流程。实验显示，恢复轨迹监督能够提高小模型在公开知识图谱任务上的求解成功率，条件绑定表示能够改善本批雷达资料问答中的参数归属与答案支持，为小模型知识问答的训练与领域应用提供了实证依据。

\noindent\textbf{关键词：}知识图谱问答；小语言模型；执行反馈；恢复轨迹监督；雷达知识；条件绑定

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Executable knowledge graph question answering connects natural-language questions with external knowledge through a sequence of query operations. During free-running execution, small language models encounter intermediate states that depart from reference trajectories. Conventional training on successful demonstrations provides limited coverage of these states, leading to repeated invalid calls and unfinished solutions. Radar documentation presents an additional challenge: technical parameters depend on equipment models, components, and operating conditions, and answers require traceable supporting sources. This thesis studies recovery-trajectory supervision based on actual execution feedback and investigates radar knowledge organization and domain question answering.

First, a source-grounded representation and evaluation collection are developed for radar documents. Records preserve subjects, components, attributes, quantity forms, applicable conditions, and source locations, including shared subjects and conditional associations in specification tables. The resulting workflow covers source archiving, record construction, question authoring, and cross-review. The collection contains 502 source readings and 96 Chinese questions from eight manufacturer source groups. AI cross-review supplies reference answers and evaluation materials for factual selection, condition preservation, and citation support.

Second, a recovery-supervision method is proposed using actual divergent states and executable continuations. An initial model runs freely on training questions, yielding 418 paired examples from failed trajectories. Each recovery example retains a shared successful prefix, the first reference-divergent action, and its execution observation. Intermediate-result handles are remapped to connect the reference suffix to the current state, execution replay validates the continuation, and the training loss is applied to subsequent solution actions. Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct is adapted with LoRA and compared with ordinary continuation training using the same initial checkpoint, questions, record order, and supervised-token budget. On a project-held-out set of 2,000 KQA Pro questions, mean accuracy across two paired continuation seeds increases from 78.75\% to 83.50\%. The gain is 4.75 percentage points, with a question-level paired bootstrap 95\% confidence interval of $[3.53,5.98]$. The two seeds yield gains of 4.65 and 4.85 percentage points and reduce cumulative input and generated tokens by 8.84\% and 4.17\% relative to their matched stepwise controls. State-transition analysis associates most of the net gain with fewer unfinished episodes, alongside a positive net improvement among completed answers.

Finally, a radar question-answering workflow integrates interface adaptation, record selection, and source-based answer generation. Shared adaptation on synthetic interface examples raises selection performance to 39--40 correct cases out of 40 for the five checkpoints. In a four-source answering study, ordinary and recovery variants obtain similar answer correctness, with 15--16 correct answers out of 24. To address parameter attribution during answer generation, a condition-bound representation is developed while preserving the same semantic records as a flat representation. On the fixed 96-question collection, answer correctness increases from 73 to 80 and binding errors decrease from 17 to 11. A posthoc analysis applying the same exact citation resolver to both representations yields joint-success counts of 55 and 68, showing the combined influence of condition organization and citation processing on evidence-supported answers.

The study connects executable training-target construction with professional knowledge organization and layered question-answering evaluation. Its experiments demonstrate improved solving success on the public knowledge graph task and improved parameter attribution and answer support within the evaluated radar collection.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} knowledge graph question answering; small language models; execution feedback; recovery supervision; radar knowledge; condition binding
